Goal: a complete signed-in flow that can be walked end to end on the Vercel Preview and matches prototype v4. Triage of 2026-11-14 (#261), made from each issue's title, labels and first lines, not a full read.

The milestone **Complete flow on Preview** holds the issues below; every other open issue is in **Later**. Those two milestones replace the `Sprint 3` to `Sprint 7` milestones on open issues. Later covers content and prompt work, caps and per-account limits, accessibility details, small style leftovers, Goals, calendar and Me details, safety and security reviews, the epics, and blocked items.
