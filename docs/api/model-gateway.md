# src/lib/model-gateway/

`src/lib/model-gateway/`: server-only `createModelGateway` and `createServerModelGateway`. The five calls are `splitPlan`, `writeStepCards`, `writeStuckStory`, `writeRestReply` and `writeReturnPopup`. There is no HTTP route for them yet. `writeStepCards` takes `StepCardsInput` = `{ minutes, facts, stepFacts?, stepNumber?, stepCount?, storyCards?, background?, mustUse?, forbidTerms?, earlierLines? }` and **no user-written text**: no Step title and no Goal (#216, US-AI-11). `facts` are who he is and where plus the Story facts of earlier Steps, and `stepFacts` the facts of this Step only (the prompt says "Already happened" and "New in this part", for CARD-NEW; an empty `stepFacts` tells the model that nothing new happens in this part, which is the case for Steps beyond the Story's facts); `stepNumber` and `stepCount` say where the Step sits in his Story; `storyCards` (1 to 5) is how many story cards follow the one opening card, and extra cards are dropped (T7; without it at most 5 cards are kept); `background` makes the first card the background card; `mustUse` are the key lines this Step must use (US-CON-2). Lengths follow CARD-LENGTH: a story card is 1 to 3 sentences and at most 45 words, the background card at most 70 words (`checks.ts`, kinds `step_card` and `background_card`). `writeStuckStory` takes `{ pauseNumber, facts, forbidTerms? }`: `pauseNumber` is the 1-based number of this Pause in the current Step (a value that is not a whole number of at least 1 is read as 1); from the third Pause the prompt asks for a much harder stuck situation in which he remembers the earlier setbacks and says he is worried he may fail, and it never tells the user what to do or asks the user to stop pausing (`docs/DECISIONS.md` F1, PAUSE-3). It takes no Step name, so no user text reaches this call (US-AI-11). It also takes `scene?: SceneTable` (`src/lib/content/scene.ts`, #64, #65, SCENE-PER-STORY): the prompt (`stuck_story` version 2) offers its `pauseProp` and lists its `props`, `present` and `allowedWords` (a line whose value is missing is left out), the `laterWords` fail the answer as `leak` when one appears as a whole word or phrase (always with its exact capitals, so "lost" is not "Los Alamos"; owner decision of 2026-11-21: the data holds no ordinary words), and `people?: string[]` (every person the Companion's Stories name, `companionPeople`) fails it as `people` when the line names one who is not in `present` or `allowedWords`, anywhere in the line; an unknown capitalised word that is no known person passes. Both rules run on every answer, so a retry names every fix. `writeReturnPopup` takes `{ trigger, facts, awayMinutes, forbidTerms? }`: `trigger` is `"rest"` (Back from the rest conversation) or `"absence"` (back after being away, R1); the prompt differs by trigger and lets him say how the time felt in words, with no clock times or numbers (TIME-WORDS). The line is 1 or 2 sentences and at most 30 words (LEN-RETURN); `checks.ts` enforces this cap in code for kind `return_popup`, so a stored prompt row that allows more cannot lengthen it (a row may only be stricter). Every line is checked by kind (`checks.ts`): step cards, the stuck story and the return pop-up allow no question and no "you"; a rest reply allows "you" and at most one question, and ends with a full stop or its one question (CHAT-QUESTION-END). `writeRestReply` takes `{ userMessage, facts, forbidTerms?, userTexts?, laterFacts?, laterWords? }` and returns the reply only, with no label (CHAT-NO-LABEL, #59): a reply that uses one of `forbidTerms` (whole words, any case) fails `leak`, except a term the person typed in one of `userTexts` (whole words, same capitals; CHAT-USER-WORD). A reply that names one of `laterFacts` (whole words, any case) or `laterWords` (whole words, exact capitals) also fails `leak` (#60, CHAT-SPOILER-CHECK), with the same exception. A failed rest reply's retry names the matched Story words (`Do not use these words: "…".`), never the person's text. The `cmd` rule on a rest reply also catches advice and a promise that it will pass ("you could", "you need to", "have you tried", "it will pass", "don't worry"), and the rest reply and the pause message also ban AI, A.I., artificial intelligence, language model and chatbot, so he never mentions being an AI (A4), even when the person asks; pre-made cards keep the shared `BANNED` list (SAFETY-PREMADE). The Story words and the CHAT-USER-WORD exception match whole words (`mentionsWord`); the exception also needs the same capitals, so "opinion" never lets "pion" through.

  **Results and failure (US-AI-8, decisions NO-BACKUP and FAIL-SCREEN).** Every call runs one pipeline: generate, parse, clean, check. An answer that fails is generated again with the failure reason added to the prompt (rule descriptions only, never user text), until it passes, `maxAttempts` (default 3) is used up, or the time limit `timeLimitMs` (default 12 000 ms, all attempts included; the real value is set by testing) runs out. A line is returned only after it passed. Success is `{ source: "generated", ... }` (`title` and `steps`, `cards`, or `text`). Failure is `{ source: "failed", reason }` with `reason` `"request"` (provider unreachable or error), `"timeout"` or `"checks"`; it carries no text, and the screen shows the calm failure message with a retry button and does not start the clock. The gateway holds no stored lines. `createServerModelGateway` takes the same `timeLimitMs` and `maxAttempts`. The log records `source` (`"generated"` or `"failed"`), `reason`, rule ids, latency and attempts.

  **Per-run list.** `writeStepCards`, `writeStuckStory` and `writeReturnPopup` take `earlierLines?: string[]`: the one-way lines already shown in this run, oldest first. The caller supplies them (for example from `session_lines`); the gateway keeps no state. A line is rejected (`repeat`) if it equals an earlier line ignoring case, punctuation and spacing; (`opening`) if its first three words equal those of one of the last four lines; (`similar`) if its content-word Jaccard similarity with any earlier line is 0.5 or more. Cards in one answer are also checked against the cards before them. `writeStuckStory` also takes `lastCard?: string`, the last card shown in the same Step; the stuck story may not reuse its nouns (`card_nouns`). Nouns are found by a heuristic: the one or two words after a determiner or possessive, plural "s" removed. The rest reply is a conversation turn and is not checked against the run list. `similarity`, `checkRunList` and `cardNouns` are exported from `run-list.ts`.
