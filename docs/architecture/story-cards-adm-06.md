# Story cards (ADM-06)

#293 (`docs/decisions/0020`): the pre-written cards of the 26 Oppenheimer stories (a background card and the cards of each part, keys `crd.opp.<story>.bg` and `crd.opp.<story>.p<n>.c<n>`) and the part titles, in `public.story_cards` (versioned, one published version per key) and `public.story_parts`. Only content editors read them through the API. ADM-06 `/admin/stories` lists the stories in story order with their parts and cards; `/admin/stories/<id>` shows the background card and the cards by part with key, slice, version and the card checks (#304, `docs/decisions/0023`, `src/lib/story-cards/checks.ts`: length, a year, date or age, words from a later part, banned words, and a card that leans on another (#16: opening with He, She, It or This, or "again", "another", "as I said", "the same", "that one"), and a person named who is not in the story's scene table (#64, SCENE-PER-STORY; every person the stories list counts, plus a title before an unlisted name; no readable scene table blocks the card)). An admin or writer saves a card as a new draft and renames parts (`src/app/admin/stories/actions.ts`, `supabase/migrations/20261123000000_story_card_editing.sql`); only the admin role publishes a draft that passes every check, or rolls back, each with a `story_card` row in Publish history. Both pages carry a notice that cards are published first and read afterwards (PUBLISH-FIRST, `src/app/admin/stories/publish-first-notice.tsx`). The pull request checks run the same card checks on the whole seed pool (`src/content/<companion>/story-decks.json`), plus a repeated-lines check in story order (repeat, same opening as one of the four cards before, near repeat), with `npm run check:cards` (`src/lib/story-cards/pool-checks.ts`, `tests/seed-card-checks.test.ts`, #46); a failing seed card fails them. The signed-in focus page reads the published cards through `get_story_cards` (#300)
