# Research flag

#114 (US-MAP-11, decisions M4, RESEARCH-FLAG, STUDY-SESSION; `docs/decisions/0031`): the internal research flag that turns the Companion and the Story off for one participant, set by the team with one SQL command (`docs/deploy.md`), never in the interface. On `/focus` the flag opens a session with `companion_id` and `story_id` `none` (or turns the open Story session Story-off in place, keeping its done Steps); such a session shows F1-STUDY (the seam, the clock, "Your goal" with the current Step and its description, Your steps, Done and Pause; no portrait, "My goal", cards, sheet or return pop-up) and R1-STUDY (the rest header only), makes no Life Path row, no summary and no Story record, and is listed in History without the Companion name. The pause message, rest reply, return pop-up and step cards actions refuse a flagged account on the server.
